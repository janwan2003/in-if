Privacy

Last updated 21 November 2026

What we store

For each plan: the text you write (plan, when, details), the number of people needed and the closing time. For each person who answers: the name they type, their answer and any “only if” condition, and a random key that lets their device change that answer. We store a one-way hash of the key, not the key itself.

Who can see it

Before a plan tips, nobody can see anyone else’s answer or condition. That includes the person who made it. Everyone who opens the plan sees how many are in and the names of the people who have opened it. After a plan tips, people with the link see the names of who’s going, and anonymous counts such as “2 were in only if Tom came”. Who said “not this time” is never shown. If a plan closes without tipping, no answers are ever shown.

What we don’t do

No accounts, no email, no phone numbers, no ads, no tracking cookies, no analytics that identify you, and we never sell or share data. Your device keeps your answer key in local storage so you don’t need to sign in.

Abuse protection

To stop one visitor using up the service, we count requests per network for an hour, using a hash of your IP address that changes every hour. These counters are deleted after the hour.

How long we keep it

Plans and their answers are deleted automatically about four months after they close. The person who made a plan can delete it at any time, and anyone can leave a plan, which removes their name and answer.

Hosting

InIf runs on Cloudflare (Pages, Functions and D1 database). Cloudflare processes requests as our hosting provider.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/janwan2003/in-if.
