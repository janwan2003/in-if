How it works

Write the plan

Say what it is and how many people it needs: “NYE house party, on if 8 are in by Sunday.” Send the link to the group.

Everyone answers in secret

Friends tap I’m in, I’m in, only if… or Not this time. Everyone sees the count, nobody sees the names.

It tips, or it quietly expires

When enough are in (conditions included), it’s on: names appear and you get a card for the chat. If not, it closes and no answer is ever shown.

Questions

Why sealed answers?

Saying yes first feels exposed, so everyone writes “maybe” and the plan dies. When nobody can see your answer unless the plan actually happens, it is safe to say what you really want. This is the same all-or-nothing idea behind crowdfunding, applied to a weekend away.

How do conditions work?

“I’m in, only if at least 8 are going” or “only if Ana and Tom are going.” InIf finds the largest group in which everyone’s conditions hold. If that group reaches the number the plan needs, it tips. After the tip, the card shows things like “2 were in only if Tom came” as counts, never who.

Who can see what?

Before the tip: everyone sees how many are in and the names of people who opened the plan, but not anyone’s answer. Not even the person who made it. After the tip: the names of who’s going. Who said “not this time” is never shown. If the plan expires, no answers are shown, ever.

Can the count give someone away?

In a very small group it can hint: if only two people have opened it and the count says 1, you can guess. For groups of five or more it works well. Each person only ever sees their own answer.

Can I change my answer?

Yes, until the deadline. After the plan tips, you can still drop out or join in, and the going list updates.

Do my friends need an app or an account?

No. They open the link, type a name and tap. Your answer is remembered on your device. You can copy a personal link to answer from another phone.

Is it free?

Yes. No ads, no tracking, no sign-up. Plans are deleted automatically a few months after they close. See the privacy policy.
